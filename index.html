Project Overview

From raw financial data to a complete investment banking case study.

For this project, I selected Celsius Holdings as the target company and completed a full valuation and transaction analysis. Instead of only filling in a template, I built the financial model structure myself, organized company and peer data, and connected the model outputs to an investment recommendation.

The project helped me practice how investment banking analysts evaluate a company: projecting performance, comparing public peers, testing capital structure, and explaining how different assumptions affect investor returns.

Key Analysis

Valuation is not one number. It is a range driven by assumptions.

The analysis showed that Celsius's valuation is highly sensitive to revenue growth, EBITDA margin expansion, and exit multiple assumptions. Because Celsius is a high growth functional beverage company, small changes in operating performance can create a meaningful impact on enterprise value.

The LBO analysis also showed the importance of capital structure. A more aggressive debt structure can increase returns when the company performs well, but it also adds risk because more cash flow must support interest and debt repayment. A conservative debt structure produces lower returns, but gives investors more stability under downside scenarios.
